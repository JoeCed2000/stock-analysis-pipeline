① **EPS: Significant miss vs. estimate, but massive YoY growth.**  
● Actual EPS of $1.76 fell well short of the $2.82 consensus estimate, a -37.6% miss.  
● YoY growth of +97.8% remains exceptional, driven by the prior year's lower base.

② **Revenue: Material miss vs. estimate, but strong YoY growth.**  
● Actual revenue of $68.1B missed the $78.8B estimate by -13.6%.  
● YoY growth of +73.2% is still robust, but the sequential growth of 22% ($10B) was below the implied ramp.  
● CFO Colette Kress cited "sizable purchase orders never materialized in the quarter due to geopolitical issues" in China as a direct headwind.  
👉 For Nami-san: The revenue miss is the core issue. The China revenue constraint is a specific, identifiable factor, not a broad demand collapse.

③ **Quality of the beat/miss: Low-quality miss — both top and bottom line disappointed.**  
● Both EPS and revenue missed estimates, indicating a broad-based shortfall.  
● The EPS miss is larger in percentage terms than the revenue miss, suggesting operating costs (e.g., rising input costs, inventory growth of 32% QoQ) also pressured margins.  
● CFO noted "input costs are on the rise" and inventory grew 32% QoQ, while supply commitments surged 63% sequentially — signaling potential margin compression ahead.  
● Gross margin guidance of "mid-seventies" implies further pressure into fiscal 2027.  
👉 For Nami-san: This is a low-quality miss. The combination of a revenue shortfall and rising costs creates a negative operating leverage effect. The China issue is a one-off, but the cost pressures are structural. Caution is warranted on near-term margin trajectory.

> One-line summary: NVDA's quarter was a clear miss on both EPS and revenue, driven by China revenue constraints and rising input costs, but YoY growth remains exceptional.
